---
title: Frontmatter controls the page title
description: Unknown frontmatter fields are allowed.
---

In Markdown, the `title` above becomes both the browser-tab title and the page
heading. In MDX, it only controls the browser-tab title, leaving visible
headings to the MDX content.

Unknown fields such as `description` remain valid, while `draft: true` excludes a
page from the build.
